About

Pedila

A simple window for your Hermes: the one on this PC (the same one Hermes Desktop uses, with the same chats) and the one on the server.

Pedila (πέδιλα) are Hermes's winged sandals: “the beautiful sandals, immortal, golden, that carried him over the sea and over the boundless earth as fast as the breaths of the wind” (Homer, Odyssey 5.44–46).

Unofficial client for Hermes Agent; not made or endorsed by Nous Research. Code under the MIT license.

Updates

Hermes

Pedila checks every 6 hours whether there's a new version. A Hermes in Docker (like a server's) can't update itself: it's updated from its server, and here there's only a notice.
